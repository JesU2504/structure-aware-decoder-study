# Status after the bounded validation gate

15 November 2026. See [the validation findings](validation_gate_2026-11-13/findings.md)
and [the overview figure](validation_gate_2026-11-13/validation_gate.png).

The declared stronger-baseline comparison and timing bridge are complete.
There are 186 audited outer model fits, 29,184 new physical evaluation shots,
3,648 separate profiling shots, and 63,552 checked correction vectors. Three
fresh interleaved timing processes completed without resource-cap censoring.
All previous artifact hashes still match. No reserved final-test parents were used.

For p95, a modest nonlinear base model outperforms the stable graph-augmented
model on the existing parent holdouts and on the calibration bridge. It selects
the measured point-best schedule in all four nontrivial quality-screened pairs;
the nonlinear graph model matches three. Only three cost orders are interval-
resolved. The full admission sweep preserves a graph-model risk/conservatism
tradeoff at 2 ms, not a matched-risk superiority claim.

Increasing BP from 30 to 100 iterations is more expensive on every bridge
configuration's measured p95 and gives no clear aggregate accuracy gain. Standard
component decomposition improves the disconnected memory reference, including
per-call gather/merge overhead.

The p95/topology compiler headline has not passed this entry gate, so a large
reserved-parent campaign was not launched. A secondary nonlinear graph signal
for mean latency is retained as exploratory. A new mean-service/throughput
experiment or a transfer/calibration/profiling contribution would require a
separate prospective utility test and a novelty check. Neither is a confirmed
paper contribution yet. Earlier frozen-model noise-transfer failures remain valid.

“find a compiler decision where size+sparsity fails and structure fixes it”
presupposes the desired positive answer. Repeatedly looking for such a case
risks an outcome-selected demonstration rather than a new test.

A systems-method claim can reopen only with genuinely new evidence: an actual
decision/workload, sourced requirements and a credible cost/quality contract.

does not meet that prerequisite. Do not repeat the failed gate unchanged.

publishable without compiler speedup or a graph-feature victory. Its novelty
and usefulness remain unverified here; a benchmark label does not establish them.

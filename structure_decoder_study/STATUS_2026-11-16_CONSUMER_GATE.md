[findings.md](consumer_gate_2026-11-16/findings.md) and the

did not connect this policy to a concrete planner trace, sourced p95/accuracy

all frozen models profile every task and add overhead. These are development
sensitivities, not production eligibility or a reproduced hardware contract.

13.257 s. Their different timing context prevents interpreting a combined ledger
as a measured compiler speedup. Historical training labels represent 630.720 s
of timed service over 504,096 samples; this is not a minimum learning cost.

The raw-data audit passed: 108 replay rows, 57 profile files, 57 independent
evaluation files, and 375 training-label files. All nine previous snapshots
remain unchanged. No new decoder calls, physical samples or fits were made.

Close this expansion and preserve the technical record. Reopening requires an

This conclusion applies to the current proposal, not every possible use of

# Supervisor review of the proposed decoder framework

17 November 2026. Independent AI research review of `expert_proposal.md`; this is not human expert validation. Read-only proposal and primary-source review. No decoder, reconstruction, search, fitting or physical-sampling experiment was run for this review.

**Verdict: conditional go for one interface-and-baseline feasibility task; hold a decoder performance campaign and any novelty claim.** Safeguarded Anderson acceleration (AA) is a coherent hypothesis. It is not yet a compelling paper contribution. The final proposal correctly acknowledges substantive BP/DIIS and dynamic-memory prior art.

## What is reasonable, and what is new

The proposed constrained residual least-squares combination is a recognizable AA/DIIS-family intervention on a fixed message map. A real evaluation of the proposal's residual, bounded coefficients, regularization and restart make a sensible numerical experiment. Keeping the channel priors and final scoring fixed also makes the intervention easier to interpret.

This differs operationally from TEMPO-BP's variable-specific online memory control: a global history combination is not the same update as changing each variable's memory coefficient from a local one-step prediction. It also differs from PriorTilt's sparse prior perturbations. These are distinctions between mechanisms, not evidence of superiority or sufficient publication novelty. [TEMPO-BP](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21086v1), [PriorTilt](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13373v1).

**The decisive adjacency:** quimb's official BP documentation already offers DIIS message extrapolation with history, mixing and conditioning parameters. Thus applying Anderson/Pulay-style history mixing to BP is already established practice in another BP setting. Classical LDPC optimization decoding also already uses Anderson acceleration. The narrower question is whether a carefully specified variant yields a useful quantum-decoding result; neither its novelty nor its usefulness is established. [quimb BP documentation](https://quimb.readthedocs.io/en/latest/autoapi/quimb/tensor/belief_propagation/d2bp/index.html), [classical LDPC ADMM-AA article](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11666833/).

The generic safeguard is also existing numerical methodology. A future paper would need a distinctive, reproducible quantum-decoding finding or mechanism, rather than present a standard safeguard plus a new application name as a new algorithm. A useful result might still support an empirical contribution; that outcome cannot be promised in advance.

## Main ways this proposal can fail

1. **Wrong objective.** A small message residual can describe a wrong-class fixed point. Syndrome validity and logical correctness are different endpoints. Our two remaining failures do not establish oscillation, slow convergence or any particular dynamical defect. The 512-shot panel also shows that BP nonconvergence alone is a poor label for final OSD failure.
2. **Unfaithful state map.** Full directed edge messages must form a closed state with a fixed phase, schedule, scaling and numerical convention. Mixing marginal LLRs separately from edge messages would create an inconsistent decoder. The root's source inspection reports that native early syndrome termination occurs before the final extrinsic-message update; the map and the externally checked hard decision must therefore specify exactly which phase they use. A time-varying scaling convention cannot silently be treated as a fixed map.
3. **Missing implementation boundary.** Existing cached final LLRs are insufficient. The root reports that the installed public interface does not expose a per-sweep message step or a supported BP-to-OSD injection point. Source instrumentation and a validated boundary are prerequisites. A Python reference versus a Python baseline can establish a mechanism, but cannot establish a native CPU speedup.
4. **An accounting advantage.** Every proposal probe, rejected probe, parity check, posterior construction, least-squares solve, restart and postprocessor call consumes budget. Global history and reductions may overwhelm an otherwise local, parallel BP implementation. Equal nominal iterations do not mean equal work, CPU time or hardware depth.
5. **Candidate-policy confounding.** Freeze whether a syndrome-valid correction found while probing a subsequently rejected AA state is eligible for return. Apply a declared eligibility/stopping/scoring policy across arms and count every evaluation. Do not quietly turn AA into a multi-candidate decoder while the plain control is required to stop at its first candidate. This issue is separate from the residual safeguard.
6. **An ordinary known heuristic explains the gain.** Fixed damping, additional sweeps or conventional DIIS may provide the same benefit. The proposal currently makes an unsafeguarded ablation optional and late; a same-map history-mixing comparator or equivalent controlled ablation is necessary before attributing any gain to the safeguard. TEMPO is a mandatory close dynamic-memory comparison before a competitive method claim.
7. **Selection and tuning.** The old 24 cases are highly selected and repeatedly inspected. Even all 512 are development data now. There is no training problem in the neural-network sense, but solver budgets, parameter selection and post-selection can still produce misleading conclusions. No per-case histories, thresholds or rescues should determine the method. All rescues and regressions count; labels and true faults remain evaluation-only.

No convergence guarantee transfers automatically from safeguarded-AA theory: the relevant operator assumptions have not been established for this min-sum map. Residual acceptance is an algorithmic check, not a decoding certificate. [Safeguarded-AA theory](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/pdf/scs_2.0_v_global.pdf).

## One concrete next task

**Produce a bounded interface-and-baseline feasibility verdict for this exact source configuration.** This is an entry test, not yet an AA performance experiment or a manuscript.

Before execution, pin the source, define the complete sweep state and hard-decision phase, document the BP-to-postprocessor boundary, choose the valid-probe policy, and set a finite engineering/resource cap. Instrument one coherent sweep without changing ordinary BP behavior. The no-acceleration mode must reproduce the source-native reference on small dependent/cyclic fixtures and a frozen panel: stop iteration, correction, final outcome and posterior precision must satisfy predeclared criteria. Existing tie/order lessons require explicit postprocessor agreement; similar LLRs alone are insufficient. Preserve every failed gate and do not remove cases to pass.

One algebraic detail needs an explicit contract: setting the history to one state in the displayed proposal gives `(1-beta)*x + beta*F(x)`. With beta=0.5 this is fixed damping, not ordinary BP. Declare a separate exact plain-BP mode (or beta=1 without mixing), an exact fixed-damping mode, and their fallback behavior. All must pass their intended baseline identities. This is a protocol gap to resolve, not evidence of an executed bug.

The proposed history, ridge, acceptance threshold, fallback, timing criterion and valid-probe chronology remain illustrative or unspecified; this review does not approve a performance protocol. Before any later pilot, also identify a genuinely uninspected confirmation source/program/noise setting that is legally and technically accessible. Merely promising an untouched task is insufficient, and additional random shots from the same fixed matrix do not establish cross-family transfer.

**Go:** the source boundary is accessible, baseline fidelity passes and total added state/work is measurable. Then request review of one frozen development pilot with ordinary BP, matched extra-work BP, fixed damping and AA, retaining the same postprocessor. Include ordinary DIIS/safeguard attribution and TEMPO before making a novelty or competitive claim. Evaluate logical error versus total cost, not residual reduction alone. Only a useful fixed-configuration signal against strong controls would justify a fresh, untouched confirmation.

**Stop:** the interface cannot be validated within the cap, the implementation changes the baseline inadvertently, or the mechanism reduces to an already implemented comparator without a specific new scientific question. A later pilot should also stop if its gain is explained by damping or extra work, if probe overhead removes it, or if final logical outcomes do not improve. Do not respond by indefinitely adding histories, tuning around the remaining two failures or enlarging the benchmark.

The existing 10/12 selected-case repair result remains a checked headroom observation for a known search. It neither motivates a specific AA failure mechanism nor validates a new decoder. The original structure-aware compiler framing remains on hold: success of this different decoder experiment would not establish a structural cost-model or compiler-decision claim.

**Publication assessment:** reasonable small feasibility exploration; high novelty risk and medium implementation risk; no publishable claim established yet. I would reject the claim “we introduce AA for BP,” but would consider a later, well-controlled quantum-decoding result on its own evidence.

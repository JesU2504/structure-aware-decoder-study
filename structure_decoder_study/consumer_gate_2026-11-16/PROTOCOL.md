# Consumer/cost gate: existing evidence only

16 November 2026. Execution of Stage 0 in the previous design. This protocol is
a post hoc analysis specification: preliminary source reads and numerical
queries already exposed the construction and quality-subset findings. It is not
a preregistration or an independent test.

No new decoder calls, physical samples, training, hyperparameters, task families
or dependencies. Do not edit earlier artifacts. The primary decision is whether
the current selective-profiling workflow has enough consumer and material-cost
evidence to advance to new timing or fresh-parent experiments.

Inspect the closest public consumers (PACE, Triage, DART-Q and the offline
decoder-choice protocol), distinguishing their actual output, budget, service
statistic and scope from ours. A related paper alone is not an established
consumer for the proposed profiling policy. Record unknown requirements rather
than supplying convenient numbers. Search results without primary support do
not establish code availability or absence.

Recover construction and training-label costs from existing records, keeping
their timing contexts separate from the bridge. Replay all six frozen linear/RBF
filters over the full inherited nine-budget grid, both on all 19 bridge tasks
and the ten tasks passing the existing retrospective logical-Z screen. The
screen is not a production oracle or an application-derived accuracy target.
Use exactly the old profile upper bounds; do not refit, recalibrate or select a
model/budget from this replay. Linear base6 remains the prior designated simple
control. Charge the available base/density feature timing to base6 as before.

Identify exact old circuit matches as potential cache opportunities only.
Neither identical files nor a prior timing measurement guarantee context validity.
Do not infer a cache-hit rate for an actual planner from this fixture.

Report historical-cost illustrations as such: inserting three old circuit-build
costs into the bridge partial ledger is not a measured end-to-end speedup or a
lower bound for an optimized future compiler. Similarly, the recorded decoder
service represented by 125 training labels is not the minimum required learning
cost or a mandatory deployment break-even point.

Then audit the numerical replay independently against raw stored timing arrays,
training target provenance, circuit hashes and every previous snapshot. Check
that the written disposition does not turn a scope mismatch, a hypothetical
cost illustration or a negative gate into a universal impossibility claim.

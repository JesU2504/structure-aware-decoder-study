# Native integration entry failed at the first fixture

18 November 2026. The fresh raw-first adapter, owned-process monitor and
independent audit were implemented and reviewed. The one permitted frozen
entry execution **failed**, stopped and was not retried or resumed.

## Observed outcome

The first integral-slack toy made one optimizer call and returned its valid
optimum. Raw solution[1,1,1,1,0] and score4 were saved before warning/status
interpretation, followed by provisional data, restored handler, stable empty
signal ledger and authoritative final report. Independent saved-output checks
confirm the exact parity/domain equations and exhaustive optimum.

The monitor then observed a raw ps record with state `?E`, zero CPU and zero
RSS while its before/after owned polls still returned None. The next owned
poll confirmed normal exit0. The prospective policy allowed only its declared
states; it rejected the unknown leading state, retained the earlier CPU/RSS
maxima and kept that monitoring failure after reaping. No termination signal
was needed. The controller stopped the remaining12 fixtures as specified.

Our manual-derived allowlist was incomplete for this environment. A normal
process exit does not retroactively meet the frozen state criterion. The
mathematically correct toy artifact does not validate the entry, process
accounting, remaining guards or timeout/signal paths under this adapter.

## Next decision

Close this bounded failed entry. Next review a separate stdlib-only process
ownership protocol with a single kernel-backed reaper obtaining exact child
exit status and final per-child resource usage. Keep interim ps telemetry
and invalid-live-measurement handling explicit. Do not mix competing reapers,
rely on aggregate child usage, or spend more optimizer calls debugging exits.
Prospectively test ordinary exit, resource stops and setup/cleanup failures
before any new optimizer integration gate.

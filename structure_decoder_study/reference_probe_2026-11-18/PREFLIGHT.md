# Independent AI supervisor preflight: reference witness probe

18 November 2026. Read-only review of the entry evidence, protocol, model/worker, controller, monitor and independent audit before freezing the two real solves. This is internal AI review, not human peer review. This reviewer executed no solver, scientific calculation or adapter.

**GO for the fixed two-case, one-sided witness probe, conditional on freeze/integrity checks and independent post-run audit.** This is a justified diagnostic of representation headroom, not an online decoder experiment or a lower-bound certificate project. No new method, error-rate improvement or publication claim is approved.

## Entry decision

An installed public optimizer is sufficient for the positive-witness branch. The saved entry evidence reports normal completion of four tiny parity-MIP fixtures checked against exhaustive binary enumeration, including integral-slack, dependent/cyclic, zero-syndrome and infeasible cases. SciPy/HiGHS versions and the installed backend binary are recorded. The entry-tested source snapshot is preserved; the controller compares the four scientific helper functions and their options/constants before freeze. Additional saved return-validation adapters cover invalid proximity, parity, bounds, nonfinite values, shape, missing incumbents and valid rounding.

This does not validate a rigorous real-case lower bound. Numerical solver bounds, optimal status and infeasible status cannot establish the negative branch here. An independently checked feasible binary correction can establish existence without trusting optimality. Therefore an available feasible-witness-only branch meets the entry condition; no witness leaves the question unresolved.

## Scientific design checked

- Exactly cases 131 and 228 are attempted, once each, in that order. All 12,240 fault variables are retained. The model uses the unchanged source float64 `log(1/p)` weights and all 1,800 H plus 12 actual-observation L equations. Both binary fault variables and parity-slack variables are integral; the slack bounds preserve the full parity-feasible set.
- A saved correct-class vector is validated against the entire model before solving. The current saved full-1FV wrong correction is detector-valid but rejected by the logical constraint. Its score, approximately 520.87671 or 450.11581, is the threshold; the older OSD0 correction is not substituted.
- There is no objective cutoff, restricted subgraph, new noise model, alternate score, per-case parameter tuning or additional class search. Actual logical outcomes are supplied as an oracle for this post hoc analysis and cannot be used by a practical online decoder.
- Returned raw arrays are preserved before validation. Finite values must meet integer proximity before rounding; the resulting vector and auxiliary integers must satisfy all exact binary/domain/parity equations. No parity repair is performed. A valid incumbent from a time-limited solve can establish existence.
- Both candidate and wrong-reference costs are recomputed with the same frozen coefficients using independent summation paths and exact rational sums of their floating representations. Improvement must exceed the frozen tolerance. This verifies the represented objective, not exact real logarithms or the physical posterior.
- The independent audit imports no optimizer/model helper. It checks H/L feasibility, auxiliary domains, raw/rounded identity, source-reference identity, exact represented costs and outside-list status. A positive witness must also beat the best old correct-class representative. It cannot establish global decoder success, since competing wrong classes might also improve.

## Bounded execution and fixes reviewed

Each solver has 60 seconds and 10,000 nodes, fixed one-thread/seed/presolve/tolerance options; each child has 90 CPU seconds, 120 wall seconds and 1 GiB RSS operational limits. The independent audit has its own declared cap. Four entry optimizations and up to two real optimizations are counted explicitly; zero BP/OSD/LSD calls does not mean zero additional decoding-related computation.

Pre-freeze review prompted these operational improvements, now present in the reviewed code:

1. Unexpected real solver statuses and optimal-without-incumbent stop validation; the known feasible vector makes an infeasibility claim contradictory. Typed resource causes are preserved separately.
2. Raw arrays and solver metadata are saved before potentially rejecting an incumbent.
3. Invocation auditing accepts an ordered subsequence of attempted cases, so a first-case resource stop before its solve can legitimately be followed by the second case. Duplicate/out-of-order calls and return-before-start are rejected.
4. The normal no-incumbent branch must match status 1, raw metadata, absent raw array and the unresolved classification.

These changes concern reporting and auditing; the entry-tested scientific helper functions remain subject to an exact source-identity gate. No real optimization outcome has been inspected at this preflight.

## Stop/go interpretation

A verified cheaper correct-class vector establishes only an outside-list competitive representative for that observation under this score. No validated cheaper vector means unresolved, including at a solver limit or numerical optimality claim. A formulation/validation error stops further interpretation; partial and rejected work must be retained. Do not change caps or methods after observing the outcome.

Require the completed independent audit and supervisor postcheck before reporting conclusions. This probe does not automatically authorize AA, rescoring, a solver expansion or a performance campaign. Task-specific publication remains contingent on an original useful mechanism/intervention and later fresh same-task evidence.

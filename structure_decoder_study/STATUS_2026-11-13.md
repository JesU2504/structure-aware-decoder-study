# Current status: 13 November 2026

The frozen-model stress exploration is complete. See
[the failure-boundary findings](stress_gate/findings.md) and
[the failure map](stress_gate/results/failure_map.png).

There are 56 new connected circuit configurations from 12 new parent programs,
82,968 new physical observations, and 48 selected mechanism replays. The old
905-file artifact remains unchanged. New raw data, hypotheses, protocols,
predictor coefficients, selection rules, and internal audits are retained in
`stress_gate/`.

Two historical-margin timing-admission errors repeated on fresh samples at
higher noise. Intermediate-rate tests bracket the selected tasks' change from
below to above the illustrative 5% deadline-miss limit while H/L remain fixed.
Selected slow shots are dominated by LSD work. Thirty-six initial discovery cases
inside the old marginal feature ranges did not show a comparable prediction
failure, but a subsequent targeted feature-box challenge did. Derived prior/
activity densities reveal a shift that raw sums/counts alone can conceal.

The main inference is a calibration/domain-validity problem, with expensive LSD
work as the mechanism. A missing static-topology feature has not been established.
The confirmed counterexamples have poor logical-Z accuracy, so a useful FTQC
compiler contribution remains unproven. The next candidate experiment should
calibrate independent noise/size variation and test new reserved programs before
adding graph-model complexity or claiming a paper contribution.

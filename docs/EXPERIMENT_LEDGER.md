## Authoritative evidence hierarchy

1. For the public mirror, `PUBLIC_EXPORT.json` establishes exported bytes.
2. Within an experiment, read its frozen protocol/configuration, raw data,
   invocation/phase journals, process outcomes and independent audit together.
3. Read completed RESULTS and postchecks for scope/known failures.
4. Dated status summaries help navigation; an earlier suggestion is not a
   completed result or authority to launch a new campaign.

Historical manifests contain original host paths and hashes. Privacy
transformation can change text bytes. They are retained for provenance, not
as executable portable manifests for this mirror. Do not edit their hashes
to manufacture a match. New work needs fresh paths and a fresh freeze.

## Things a new collaborator must not accidentally claim

- A syndrome-valid correction is not necessarily logically correct.
- The12 selected success controls do not establish behavior on the other488.
- Correct-class membership is not competitive class coverage or posterior mass.
- A true-class-constrained oracle solve is not an online decoding method.
- No witness, infeasible status or a floating dual estimate does not prove
  that no competitive correction exists in the real task.
- A valid vector from a censored process does not make its runtime a clean
  budget-feasibility observation.
- AI review is not independent human expert validation.
- Runner tests are not decoder accuracy experiments or evidence of novelty.
